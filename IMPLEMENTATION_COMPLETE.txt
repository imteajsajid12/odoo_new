================================================================================
                        TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
================================================================================

ISSUE 1: Trainer field not visible in form
SOLUTION: Hard refresh browser (Cmd+Shift+R or Ctrl+Shift+R)
ALTERNATIVE: Clear browser cache and cookies

ISSUE 2: Cannot select trainer
SOLUTION: Ensure Contacts app is installed and activated
CHECK: Settings > Apps > Search "Contacts"

ISSUE 3: Warning alert always showing
SOLUTION: Verify individual contacts exist in system
CHECK: Contacts > Contacts > Filter by "Individual"

ISSUE 4: Changes not saved
SOLUTION: Ensure you have write permissions
CHECK: Settings > Users & Companies > Your User > Access Rights
